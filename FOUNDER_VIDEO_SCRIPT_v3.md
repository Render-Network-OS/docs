## BEAT 1 — WHO I AM (~35s)

**[VISUAL: Face to camera. Clean frame. No set dressing.]**

i'm enoomian. and i am obsessed with solving difficult problems.

i've been writing code since i was sixteen. over ten years of building things that weren't supposed to exist yet. my first industrial project was an expert system for the World Health Organization — before AI was a buzzword — improving quality of life during maternal and perinatal admissions. reducing mortality rates during childbirth. that program ran for two years.

after that i chased problems wherever they lived. embedded systems for supermarket inventory. home security protocols with Siemens. programming unmanned machines for Caterpillar in C and C++. experiential learning platforms in EduTech. credit systems on the blockchain.

i don't debug. i architect. and when the problem is big enough, i build the entire system myself.

---

## BEAT 2 — THE PROBLEM (~30s)

**[VISUAL: Face to camera. Slower pace. Weight on every line.]**

my current obsession is the Creator Economy. and it has some of the most sophisticated cross-domain misalignment i've ever seen.

the problem sits at the intersection of entertainment, advertising, finance, and automation. platforms monetize a hundred percent of users but pay only the top ten to twenty-five percent. everyone else — the people whose content keeps users on the platform, whose attention generates the ad revenue — gets nothing.

and the audiences? they're the product. they generate billions of dollars in value annually and receive zero compensation.

that's broken. and it's a problem worth solving.

---

## BEAT 3 — THE THESIS (~25s)

**[VISUAL: Face to camera. Core conviction.]**

so i built Render Network. i've been building it for over six months to help creators own, measure, and transfer the value that they create.

the thesis is simple: attention is the scarcest resource in the universe, and the people who generate it and the people who give it should both get paid.

but i'm not building for the Attention Economy. i'm building for what comes after it — the Abundance Economy. a future where AI and autonomous systems have equipped everyone to create value. and there is no structure capturing the ownership, measure, and transfer of that value. that's the missing piece. that's what i'm building.

---

## BEAT 4 — WHAT'S LIVE (~50s)

**[VISUAL: Screen capture of stream.rndrntwrk.com — show the studio, the simulcast panel, the overlay controls.]**

towards solving this, i first built 555. 555 is the denominator. the unit of value. every economic activity in the ecosystem is measured through 555.

then i built 555stream — a browser-based live streaming studio. no download. no desktop app. just a browser tab. you open it, and you're live on seven platforms simultaneously — Twitch, YouTube, Kick, X, TikTok, Zora, and pump.fun. encoding runs in the cloud. your machine doesn't touch it.

**[VISUAL: Show L-Bar ad overlay in action — the squeeze-back format, program audio continuing.]**

monetization is non-interruptive. L-Bar ads — squeeze-back overlays like ESPN during live sports. your content never stops. an AI engine auto-triggers ads at optimal engagement moments so the creator doesn't have to think about it.

**[VISUAL: Show arcade games, leaderboard.]**

we have eighteen browser games with a shared leaderboard. score-backed progression — skill is rewarded, not luck, not wealth.

**[VISUAL: Show sw4p interface or audit badges.]**

sw4p — our cross-chain bridge — is audited by Zigtur. five separate audits across EVM, Solana, and Tron. live on mainnet.

and Alice — our AI agent — operates the stream autonomously. she manages the broadcast, triggers ads, controls overlays, invites guests. she's not a chatbot. she's a proprietor.

---

## BEAT 5 — THE ECONOMIC DESIGN (~30s)

**[VISUAL: Simple diagram — revenue flow. ARP at the top.]**

the economic design is deliberate. ten percent of every dollar goes to the Audience Reward Pool before anything else. policy-enforced. audiences aren't the product — they're participants with economic rights.

post-ARP, revenue splits between creators and the platform. the platform's share funds token burns, reserves, and protocol development. every split is published. every credit is on the ledger.

$555 — the token — has a one billion supply. ninety-two percent public distribution. no VC. no seed round. no private sale. my eight percent is on a five-year lock. and twenty percent of platform revenue buys and burns $555 on the market. structural deflation, not marketing.

---

## BEAT 6 — THE THREE STAKEHOLDERS (~20s)

**[VISUAL: Face to camera.]**

this ecosystem needs three contributors. creators attract audiences. audiences generate inventory — verifiable attention that advertisers want to reach. and advertisers provide the capital that keeps the economy functioning.

no single contributor is more important than the other. aligning these incentives has been a cross-domain problem between entertainment, automation, finance, and advertisement. every wall i hit forced me to build something bigger. every problem revealed a deeper problem. that's how a streaming tool became a protocol.

---

## BEAT 7 — WHY BET ON ME (~30s)

**[VISUAL: Face to camera. Direct. Quiet conviction.]**

problems are exhaustive. but not a lot of people have what it takes to go months without giving up on one. especially when most of the odds are against them.

i spent 2024 and 2025 on sabbatical building agents. memory systems for agents. persistent sessions. giving autonomous systems an edge. and now i'm bringing those agents to the internet to participate alongside humans in the economy that comes next.

everything that has been built on Render Network — the streaming studio, the bridge, the games, the AI agent, the economic layer — was built by one person. eleven repositories. Go, Rust, TypeScript, Solidity, Solana Anchor. i design and build the entire stack myself.

i'm going to build Render Network regardless. but betting on me means you're betting on a stake in the future. you're betting on someone who stays on a problem until it's exhausted.

---

## BEAT 8 — THE CLOSE (~15s)

**[VISUAL: Face to camera. Slow. Final.]**

i'm building towards a future where everyone is creating, and everyone can own and transfer some of that value. where audiences aren't the product — they're participants. where the internet actually pays attention to attention.

Render Network is building the economic operating system for human and agent media — where all creators, human and autonomous, can add value, own the value they add, and transfer that measurable value towards a sustainable economy.

five five five is words in the room.
