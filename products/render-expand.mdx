## Overview

Render Expand is the planned path for creators to develop creator-specific coordination and economic surfaces within RNDRNTWRK.

## Planned Flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the Creator Experience">
    Describe its purpose, the people involved, and how they would participate. If the experience uses a creator token, define its name, symbol, purpose, and supply approach alongside those participation rules.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the Terms">
    Explain access, funding, ownership, and how contributors would be paid. Any token commitment, lock period, release condition, or role in decisions would need its own clear terms.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the Products">
    Identify the creator tools, media formats, and interactive experiences the programme would use. SW4P provides the settlement role; SW4P Earn is the model for allocations, rewards, claims, payout schedules, reserves, and reconciliation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explain the Experience">
    Describe what people would be joining, which features are included, and where they can read the terms. A creator profile could also explain the formats and placements available to commercial partners.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the Parts Would Connect

The plan brings together creator-token design, stream controls, participation, and economic arrangements.

Creator tools would present the experience and its access conditions. SW4P would coordinate settlement where payment is involved. Any staking-related allocation, reward, claim, payout, reserve, or reconciliation capability would belong to SW4P Earn.

## Development Direction

The design questions include how a creator token would be created, how access rules would be presented, and how participants would understand their commitments and any role in decisions.

A future experience would describe its chosen launch method and supported integrations.

## Funding and Discovery

A creator experience could include sponsored streams, placements, or other commercial collaborations. Its description would explain the work offered, the audience or community involved, and the basis for any reported results.

Programme funding, service fees, and token-related revenue remain distinct. The experience's agreements would identify its costs, recipients, and allocations.

## Relationship to SW4P Earn

SW4P Earn extends SW4P’s economic model into source-aware allocation, claims, rewards, payout schedules, reserves, and reconciliation.

**Current status: SW4P Earn is not yet publicly available. No staking, liquidity, reward, or claim programme is currently open.**

A future experience would publish its funding, allocation, and participation terms before people join. No APY, yield, return, automatic reward, buyback, or claim is guaranteed.
