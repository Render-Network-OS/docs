# SW4P Earn

**SW4P Earn extends SW4P’s economic model into source-aware allocation, claims, rewards, payout schedules, reserves, and reconciliation.**

SW4P Earn keeps programme rules clear: where value came from, how it is allocated, who can claim it, what is reserved, what has been paid, and what remains to reconcile.

## What it covers

| Capability | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Source tracking** | Keeps fees, funded incentives, reserves, and other programme funds distinct. |
| **Allocation** | Applies the programme's stated rules to eligible activity and recipients. |
| **Claims** | Shows who can claim, under which programme, and when. |
| **Payout schedules** | Connects a claim to its timing and payout state. |
| **Reserves** | Separates reserved value from value available for allocation or payout. |
| **Reconciliation** | Records what was allocated, paid, returned, or remains outstanding. |

## Current status

**SW4P Earn is not yet publicly available. No staking, liquidity, reward, or claim programme is currently open.**

## When a Programme Is Available

Before participating, users will see the eligible activity, funding source, allocation rules, recipients, claim timing, supported network and addresses, effective date, and support path.

Fee-derived allocations and separately funded incentives remain separate. No APY, yield, return, automatic reward, buyback, or claim is guaranteed.
