This page explains how subscriptions, token-based access, and staking could form part of a future programme, and how those arrangements relate to SW4P Earn.

**Current status: SW4P Earn is not yet publicly available. No staking, liquidity, reward, or claim programme is currently open.**

## Access and Subscriptions

Getting started with RNDRNTWRK requires an internet-enabled device.

A subscription is an arrangement for access to a product or service over a stated period. Its terms explain what is included, the price, renewal, and cancellation. A particular experience may instead use a token holding as an access condition; its terms would identify the token, network, amount, and access provided.

Product or programme terms describe these arrangements wherever they are offered.

## Staking Within a Programme

Staking would mean committing tokens under a programme's rules. Those rules would explain what the commitment supports, how long it lasts, when tokens can be released, and the costs and risks involved.

Any staking-related allocation, reward, claim, payout schedule, reserve, or reconciliation capability belongs to SW4P Earn. A programme would also explain participants’ role in making decisions.

## Before Participating

A future programme would explain:

- who can take part and which activity is eligible;
- what funds it, keeping fee-derived allocations separate from funded incentives;
- how allocations are calculated and who receives them;
- any token commitment, lock period, release conditions, and costs;
- reward and claim timing, supported network and addresses;
- its effective date and support path.

Its description would distinguish points or balances used within an experience from anything that can be transferred, claimed, or paid out. No APY, yield, return, automatic reward, buyback, or claim is guaranteed.
