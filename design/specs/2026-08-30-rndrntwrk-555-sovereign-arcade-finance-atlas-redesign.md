# RNDRNTWRK / SW4P / $555 Sovereign Arcade Finance Atlas Redesign

**Status:** Approved creative direction; written specification awaiting final review  
**Date:** 2026-08-30  
**Target:** 56-page public PDF, editable PowerPoint, reusable SVG figure pack  
**Approved direction:** **$555 Sovereign Arcade Finance**

## Decision

The v5 atlas will be rebuilt rather than cosmetically reskinned. The current generic fintech language, repeated card layouts, ordinary office-style charts, generic donuts and weak logo treatment will be replaced by a bespoke $555 system based on the supplied crowned pixel mark.

Pixel geometry establishes identity, hierarchy and world-building. Quantitative geometry remains exact, legible, data-bound and source-linked. The finished artifact must feel like a sovereign arcade economy without becoming a game UI or compromising analytical seriousness.

- Canonical visual reference: user-supplied crowned $555 logo, 2048×819, SHA-256 `f46956f993b251fd6e3535386e2d06fb2d3ad53e5fa5a46844232041c5747d09`.
- Superseded visual baseline: v5 public atlas PDF, SHA-256 `077e85646d6970594718f51c0f8ac2b57761dd8c0ce464190fa4142522c3cdc0`.
- Binding content baseline: v5 approved master copy, SHA-256 `57f30d876f824b6ea28097358e9d55e693259246b719bd7d152a8a56512c70f7`.

- Representative concept gate: pages 1, 14, 15 and 28.

## Objectives

1. Make the atlas unmistakably $555 even with the logo hidden.
2. Preserve every approved economic claim and source relationship.
3. Use the correct visualization form and renderer for each analytical job.
4. Create genuine chapter pacing instead of repeating one slide template.
5. Keep titles, annotations, source rails and important labels editable in PowerPoint.
6. Export reusable SVG assets and source data for the reports site.
7. Survive projection, print, grayscale, accessibility review and static reproduction.
8. Make regeneration deterministic when metrics change.

## Non-goals

- No new public metrics or changes to the approved economic corpus.
- No pixel borders pasted around generic charts.
- No generated imagery used as evidence.
- No provider treatment that implies partnership or endorsement.
- No full-deck production before the four representative concepts are approved.
- No distribution of font files.

## Binding creative direction

> $555 is the crowned economic asset at the center of a programmable digital economy.

The system expresses sovereignty through crown geometry, centered hierarchy and monumental scale; scarcity through restrained gold; programmability through grids, rails, gates and state transitions; arcade origin through stepped geometry and score logic; financial seriousness through exact units and strong encodings; onchain permanence through transaction strips and visible supply removal; expansion through outward routes and capacity gates; and compounding through causal loops and accumulators.

### Locked decisions

- The supplied crowned mark is canonical.
- Gold means $555 principal, treasury capital or activated allocation.
- Ember means permanent supply destruction.
- Violet means locked, vested, conditional or programmable inventory.
- Cyan means movement, routing, settlement or infrastructure.
- Green means settled receipts and positive contribution.
- Crown use is restricted to pages 1, 5, 9, 18, 26, 36, 44, 51, 53 and 54.
- Native PowerPoint charts are prohibited for substantial figures.
- Generic donuts, 3D bars, dual axes, perspective-distorted charts and decorative gradients are prohibited.
- The current default right-side explanation panel is retired.
- Every quantitative figure is data-bound and source-linked.

- Base pixel: 4 px.
- Presentation unit: 8 px.
- Hero step: 16 px.
- Standard outline: 2 px; hero outline: 4 px.
- Allowed corners: square, 4 px notch, 8 px notch.
- Stepped shadows only; no blurred shadows.
- Dither fields limited to material surfaces.
- Working canvas: 1920×1080.
- Safe area: 96 px left/right, 80 px top, 56 px bottom.
- Twelve columns, 24 px gutters, 8 px baseline.
- Title zone y=80–220; figure y=240–920; annotation y=940–1008; source rail y=1016–1056.

Every page has one dominant claim, one dominant visual, one subordinate context layer and one source rail.

## Accessibility and responsive translation

- Body text remains ≥17 pt.
- Color never carries meaning alone.
- Every substantial figure includes alt text.
- Grayscale preserves the main comparison.
- Source URLs remain clickable/selectable.
- The reports-site version uses methodology popovers on desktop and bottom drawers on mobile.
- Locks support historical/current views; cost figures support fixed/variable/restricted states; infrastructure supports workload-state selection.
- Essential values remain visible without hover.
- Static SVG/PNG fallbacks preserve every claim.

## Production gates

1. **Brand reconstruction:** vector mark, variants, pixel alphabet and background studies.

5. **Chapter production:** one chapter at a time with semantic and visual QA.

## QA and acceptance

The redesign is accepted only when the canonical mark passes silhouette comparison; the deck is recognizable with logos hidden; all figures are data-bound; four unique burn receipts plus one recap remain correct; lower bounds retain `>`; no generic donuts or substantial native PowerPoint charts remain; all SVG bounds and renderer parity pass; 56 slides equal 56 PDF pages; source ledgers remain readable; PowerPoint stays editable; the SVG pack is reusable on the reports site; and pages 1, 14, 15 and 28 are approved before full production.
