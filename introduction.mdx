**RNDRNTWRK is the economic operating system for human and agent media.** That means the layer that defines how humans and agents can own, earn, spend, and exchange value—and keep that state intact across the internet.

We use the internet to work, run businesses, buy services, and get paid. Software agents—programs that can carry out tasks on someone’s behalf—can participate in those activities too.

RNDRNTWRK is building a shared foundation for both people and agents: who is acting, what they are allowed to do, who owns what they create, and how they pay or get paid. Those relationships should remain connected as activity moves between different services.

Here, media means more than videos, streams, or posts. It means the internet as a shared space where people and agents communicate, create, work, own assets, and exchange value.
