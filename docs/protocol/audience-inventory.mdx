# What is Audience Inventory?

**Audience Inventory** is the concept of treating viewer attention as a verifiable, scarce, and tradable asset.

In the legacy model, "inventory" is just ad slots sold by a centralized platform. In the **555x402** model, inventory is **owned by the creator** and **verified by the protocol**.

## How It Works

1.  **Engagement**: A user plays a game or watches a stream.
2.  **Verification**: The **VAP** (Verifiable Attention Protocol) validates this activity via cryptographic heartbeats.
3.  **Minting**: Validated attention is minted as a **cNFT** (Compressed NFT) on Solana.
4.  **Utilization**:
    *   **Advertisers** buy this inventory to show ads.
    *   **Creators** sell this inventory for revenue.
    *   **Users** earn rewards for generating high-quality inventory.

## The Flywheel

The Audience Inventory model creates a self-reinforcing economic flywheel:

1.  **High-Fidelity Content** attracts users.
2.  **Verified Engagement** creates high-value Audience Inventory.
3.  **Advertisers** purchase this inventory, injecting liquidity.
4.  **Revenue** is distributed to Creators and Users (via $555/USDC).
5.  **Rewards** incentivize more content and engagement.
