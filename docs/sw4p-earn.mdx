# sw4p Earn

**sw4p Earn** is the staking and liquidity layer that sits on top of sw4p. Where sw4p moves USDC across chains, sw4p Earn lets $555 holders and liquidity providers earn from the trading and settlement activity routed through it. Real volume. Real TVL. Real fees. Real rewards — every one of them tagged at source so a participant can see exactly where the money came from.

sw4p Earn is not yield product. It is not a points farm. It is the participation surface for the economic side of the settlement rail — the place where the cascade meets liquidity, where structural demand for $555 expresses itself as locked supply, and where the $555/USDC pair gets the depth it needs to be useful.

## Why This Layer Exists

The RNDRNTWRK cascade routes 20% of platform revenue into buyback-and-burn. That mechanism creates structural demand. sw4p Earn is the matching structural commitment on the supply side: a place where $555 holders can lock alongside the cascade, where LPs provide the depth that real settlement requires, and where the rewards distributed to both come from real fee flow and clearly-labeled incentive budgets — never blended, never disguised.

The layer connects to the broader system in three concrete ways:

- **For $555 holders** — locked $555 earns a share of real trading fees from the $555/USDC Uniswap V3 pool, plus a multiplier on incentive emissions tied to lock duration.
- **For liquidity providers** — concentrated-liquidity LPs earn pool fees the standard Uniswap way, with an additional incentive layer for liquidity placed inside the active range.
- **For the protocol** — protocol-owned liquidity (POL) and a market-making reserve build the depth that lets sw4p settle real volume without slippage tax on participants.

## The Five Modules

sw4p Earn is composed of five modules, each with a distinct economic role. They share a common reward distributor but operate on independent accounting tracks.

| Module | Role | Source of Capital | Source of Rewards |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Global 555 Lock** | $555 holders lock for time-weighted share of fee flow | Voluntary $555 deposits | Real trading fees + incentive emissions |
| **LP Vault** | Concentrated $555/USDC liquidity inside active range | LPs supplying $555 + USDC | Uniswap V3 pool fees + incentive emissions |
| **Matched 555 Vault** | Protocol-matched LP — for every $555 locked, protocol pairs USDC | Locked $555 + protocol USDC | Pool fees + incentive emissions |
| **POL Vault** | Protocol-owned liquidity, never withdrawn | Treasury allocation | Pool fees route back to treasury / cascade |
| **MM Reserve** | Transparent market-making reserve for depth and volatility absorption | Treasury allocation | Mandate-defined accounting, fully public |

Each module reports its own TVL, its own fee accrual, and its own reward stream. The dashboard shows them side-by-side, never aggregated into a single number without the split.

## How Rewards Work

Rewards flow through a **merkle distributor** pattern. Every period, the distributor publishes a single merkle root that commits to every participant's earned amount across every module. Participants claim against that root.

What makes the design honest is the source tag. Every reward leaf carries one of two labels:

- **`REAL_FEE`** — the reward came from actual trading fees collected on the $555/USDC pool, or from settlement activity routed through sw4p.
- **`INCENTIVE`** — the reward came from a protocol incentive budget allocated for bootstrapping depth and participation.

The two are accounted separately, distributed separately, and displayed separately. A participant looking at their claim sees: "this much came from real fees, this much came from incentives." There is no blended yield number. The dashboard does not produce one.

In plain language: every reward you claim is labeled where it came from. The label is structural, not editorial. It is enforced by the contract, not by the marketing copy.

<Note>
  Incentive budgets are finite and disclosed in advance. When a budget ends, the `INCENTIVE` portion of rewards goes to zero — and the dashboard shows it going to zero, with no rebranding. The `REAL_FEE` portion continues for as long as there is real volume.
</Note>

## Lock Timelines and Multipliers

The Global 555 Lock and the Matched 555 Vault both use a duration-weighted multiplier. Longer locks earn a larger share of the period's reward pool. The schedule is fixed at deploy time and verifiable on-chain.

| Lock Duration | Reward Multiplier |
| :--- | :--- |
| Flexible (no lock) | 0.5x |
| 30 days | 1.0x |
| 90 days | 1.5x |
| 180 days | 2.5x |
| 365 days | 4.0x |

Multipliers apply to the participant's share of the period's reward pool, not to the absolute reward amount. They concentrate reward weight toward longer-aligned participants without altering the size of the pool itself.

## Public Proof

Before any reward campaign opens, sw4p Earn publishes its operating numbers to a public dashboard. The dashboard is the canonical surface for verifying what the system actually does.

The dashboard reports, per module:

- Total Value Locked
- Trailing volume routed through the pool
- Fees collected — separated `REAL_FEE` from `INCENTIVE`
- Active reward split — never a single blended APY without the source breakdown
- Lock-duration distribution
- POL and MM Reserve balances and any active mandates

<Note>
  The dashboard never shows a single blended APY without showing the split. This is enforced at the UI layer and verifiable on-chain — the same data that the contract emits is what the dashboard renders.
</Note>

The dashboard surface lives at `earn.sw4p.io` (planned). Until launch, the implementation lives in the worktree and is reproducible from the on-chain test environment.
