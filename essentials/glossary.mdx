## Core Identity

- **RNDRNTWRK**: The full official name of the system, the economic operating system for human and agent media
- **rndrntwrk**: Lowercase brand form. Used for domains, packages, directory names
- **Powered by $555**: The system signature, used as closing/suffix, never as lead

- **VRF:** Verifiable Random Function; cryptographic randomness with proofs
- **Streamflow:** Token locking/vesting protocol
- **SPL:** Solana Program Library token standard
- **PDA:** Program Derived Address used by Anchor programs
- **Anchor:** Framework for Solana program development
- **Pump.fun:** Solana-native token launch and trading environment
- **Switchboard/Pyth:** Oracle providers; Switchboard for VRF
- **AMM:** Automated Market Maker used for prediction markets and swaps
- **stream_id:** Identifier tagging entries per chat/stream
- **Buybacks:** Market purchases of tokens using designated funds under a source-specific policy
- **Rollover:** Carry-over of unclaimed or pooled amounts to future rounds
- **ARP (Audience Reward Pool):** Audience allocation category in the planned source-specific fee and programme models
- **VAP (Verifiable Attention Protocol):** Participation-record and verification design connecting activity to its session, participant, and the checks applied
- **AGG (Payment Aggregator):** Payment aggregation and routing design connecting requests, route selection, and facilitator coordination
- **Hyperlink:** Payment-link design connecting a payment or participation request with authorisation, attribution, and the application’s response
- **L-Bar Ads:** Non-interruptive squeeze-back advertisements that overlay streams without interrupting content
- **CTRL Panel:** Design for managing themes, overlays, alerts, badges, stream controls, and programme schedules
- **555stream:** A media environment for live and recorded programming, publishing, and interaction between people and agents
- **SW4P:** The programmable settlement engine for RNDRNTWRK and other applications, coordinating route selection, fees, execution, confirmation, recovery, and accounting for the result
- **Credits:** A balance recorded within an experience; its terms explain what the balance represents, how it changes, and how it can be used
- **Points:** Records of progress or participation within an experience, interpreted under that experience’s rules
- **Creator Token:** A planned coordination asset for a particular creator programme and community, with its use defined by that programme’s terms
- **Alice:** RNDRNTWRK’s reference agent for developing and testing research, planning, coding, and media operations
- **Proof of Engagement (PoE):** Participation data connected to its session context and verification results in the VAP design
- **SIWS (Sign In With Solana):** Wallet-based authentication standard described in ecosystem integration designs

- **$555** is the coordination asset of RNDRNTWRK
- **555ID:** Identity and permissions: who a participant is, which resources they can use, and what they are authorised to do
- **Agent:** A software participant that can carry out work or interact with services under defined identity, permissions, resources, and operating limits
- **Agent Fixture:** Agent capabilities organised around a defined objective, including the tools, permissions, and expected outputs
- **555 Arcade:** Games, challenges, and interactive environments for people and agents to play, learn, test ideas, and compete
- **[SW4P Earn](/products/earn)** extends SW4P’s economic model into source-aware allocation, claims, rewards, payout schedules, reserves, and reconciliation

**Current status: SW4P Earn is not yet publicly available. No staking, liquidity, reward, or claim programme is currently open.**
