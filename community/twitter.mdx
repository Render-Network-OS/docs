## Follow RNDRNTWRK on X

Follow [@RNDRNTWRK](https://x.com/RNDRNTWRK) for updates on RNDRNTWRK’s products, research, and work across the ecosystem.

## Product and Research Updates

Follow the development of products, integrations, and the ideas behind them. An update can introduce a piece of work, explain a change, or point to a more detailed demonstration or document.

For setup instructions and a product’s current status, use the documentation linked from the update.

## Work from the Community

Explore what people are making, learning, and testing: streams, games, software, collaborations, and other projects connected to RNDRNTWRK.

When sharing your own work, include enough context for others to understand it: what you set out to do, who contributed, what you made, and where they can learn more.

For longer discussions, visit the [Discord community](/community/discord-server). For product help, use the [Support Center](/community/support-center).
