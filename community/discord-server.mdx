## Join the Conversation

Use RNDRNTWRK’s Discord to discuss what you are building, ask questions, and share ideas with other participants.

[Join RNDRNTWRK Discord](https://discord.gg/35UNPtu8M5)

## Find the Right Conversation

Start with the server’s channel descriptions and pinned resources. Look for the conversation that fits your question or activity:

- **Community:** meet other participants, exchange ideas, and find collaborators.
- **Support:** describe a product or setup problem and find the relevant guidance.
- **Games and interactive experiences:** compare approaches, discuss results, and organise community activities.
- **Building and operating:** discuss integrations, agent workflows, media production, and the work behind a project.

For a product problem, the [Support Center](/community/support-center) explains what information to include.

## Get Oriented

Read the server’s rules and welcome information. Follow any steps shown for selecting roles or joining particular channels, and check pinned announcements for the context of an ongoing discussion.

When introducing yourself, share what you are interested in and what you hope to contribute or learn. For example:

> Hi, I’m [name]. I’m working on or exploring [project or interest]. I’d like to learn about [topic] and connect with people interested in [activity].

You can join a conversation as a creator, player, developer, operator, collaborator, or someone learning about the system.

Keep private account information, credentials, and stream keys out of public messages. Use the product documentation for its setup instructions and current status.
