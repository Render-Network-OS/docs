$555 is the coordination asset of RNDRNTWRK. This guide explains its role, how it differs from points and planned creator tokens, and how RNDRNTWRK’s economic policies connect to it.

## The Role of $555

The token is intended to coordinate selected forms of participation across RNDRNTWRK. Its intended uses include access, liquidity, incentives, curation, bonding, inventory, operator alignment, governance, and treasury activity.

Each use needs its own product rules: who can participate, what the token does, and which commitments or permissions are involved. These intended roles are not a list of features available everywhere today.

## Tokens, Points, and Settlement Assets

| Item | Role |
| --- | --- |
| **$555** | RNDRNTWRK’s coordination asset. |
| **Creator tokens** | A planned way to coordinate participation within a particular creator programme. |
| **Points and credits** | Records within an experience, interpreted under that experience’s terms. |
| **Settlement assets** | The assets used to complete a payment under the relevant payment terms. |

Points are not $555. An experience’s terms explain any conversion, transfer, or payment associated with its points or credits.

## How Revenue Connects to $555

RNDRNTWRK’s model separates programme funding, service fees, settlement revenue, and token-related revenue. Each source has its own costs, obligations, recipients, and platform share.

RNDRNTWRK’s planned treasury policy assigns 20% of eligible recognised platform revenue to $555 buybacks after the source’s senior obligations have been met. The rest of the allocation depends on the source and stage of the programme.

This is a planned use of eligible revenue, not a statement that purchases are currently happening or a promise of returns to token holders. [Fee Distribution](/tokenomics/fee-distribution) explains the allocation bases and exclusions.

## Participation Programmes

SW4P coordinates settlement. SW4P Earn extends SW4P’s economic model into source-aware allocation, claims, rewards, payout schedules, reserves, and reconciliation.

**Current status: SW4P Earn is not yet publicly available. No staking, liquidity, reward, or claim programme is currently open.**

A future programme would explain its eligible activity, funding, allocation rules, recipients, claim timing, supported network and addresses, effective date, and support path before people participate.
