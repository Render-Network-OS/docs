$555 is the coordination asset of RNDRNTWRK.

## Participation and Product Rules

A product or programme defines any role it gives to $555. Its terms explain what participants can do, what holding or committing the token means, and when those rules apply.

Intended uses include access, curation, operator alignment, governance, liquidity, and treasury activity. A token holding alone does not establish those rights across RNDRNTWRK.

## Points Are Separate

Points record activity within an experience. Credits record a balance under that experience’s terms.

Points are not $555. A credit balance represents what the experience’s terms define. Any conversion, transfer, or payment follows those rules.

## Economic Programmes

Programme-specific staking, allocations, rewards, claims, payouts, reserves, and reconciliation belong to SW4P Earn.

**Current status: SW4P Earn is not yet publicly available. No staking, liquidity, reward, or claim programme is currently open.**

A future programme would explain the activity, funding, allocation rules, recipients, claim timing, supported network and addresses, effective date, and support path before participation.

## Treasury Policy

RNDRNTWRK’s planned treasury policy connects eligible platform revenue to $555 buybacks. [Fee Distribution](/tokenomics/fee-distribution) explains the source-specific model; [Locking & Buybacks](/tokenomics/locking-and-buybacks) distinguishes purchases, burns, and token commitments.

The policy does not grant token holders a share of revenue or guarantee market purchases, liquidity, price, yield, or return.
