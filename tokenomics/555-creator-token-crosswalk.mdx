$555 and creator tokens serve different scopes. $555 is the coordination asset of RNDRNTWRK; a creator token is a planned way to organise participation within one creator programme.

## Two Scopes of Coordination

| Question | $555 | Planned creator token |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What does it relate to? | Selected forms of participation across RNDRNTWRK. | A particular creator’s programme and community. |
| Where are its roles defined? | The rules of each product or programme that uses $555. | The terms of that creator programme. |
| What might those roles include? | Access, curation, operator alignment, governance, or other product-specific utility. | Programme-specific access, commitments, or participation in decisions. |

A creator token would not replace $555’s network-wide role. Each programme would explain whether it uses either asset and for what purpose.

## Funding and Allocations

Programme funding, customer payments, and token-related revenue stay distinguishable in the records. The source and agreements determine costs, recipients, and the platform’s share.

Each creator programme would publish the funding and payment terms that govern it. Staking, allocations, rewards, claims, payout schedules, reserves, and reconciliation would belong to SW4P Earn.

**Current status: SW4P Earn is not yet publicly available. No staking, liquidity, reward, or claim programme is currently open.**

Before joining a future programme, participants would be able to read what is funded, who can take part, what commitments are involved, and how any payments or claims would work.
