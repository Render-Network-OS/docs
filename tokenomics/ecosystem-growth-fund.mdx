<Info>**Planned concept.** The Ecosystem Growth Fund describes a possible way to support ecosystem work. It is not an open funding offer.</Info>

## Purpose

The proposed fund would support work that helps people build and operate within RNDRNTWRK: creator programmes, tools, integrations, shared infrastructure, and security.

Its purpose is to connect funding to a defined contribution and make the use of that funding understandable.

## Work It Could Support

Potential areas include:

- **Creator and contributor programmes:** work with a defined purpose, participants, and deliverables.
- **Tools and integrations:** software or modules that other builders can use.
- **Shared infrastructure:** media, identity, agent, or settlement capabilities that support more than one activity.
- **Security and reliability:** work that helps protect and maintain the systems people depend on.

The scope and terms of any future funding offer would identify which work is eligible.

## Funding and Commitments

A proposed award would need a named funding source and an amount available for that purpose. The records would distinguish money spent on work from capital committed to liquidity, settlement inventory, or reserves.

RNDRNTWRK’s source-aware economic model determines which funds remain available after their costs and obligations. Any allocation to the fund would be identified by source and purpose.

## How a Future Offer Would Work

Before accepting applications or contributions, a funding offer would explain:

- the work it supports and who can apply;
- the funding source, available amount, and intended recipients;
- how proposals are assessed and who makes the decision;
- the work, milestones, or other conditions attached to support;
- payment timing and how progress and spending are reported; and
- the contact or support path.

## Payments and Programme Records

The fund describes a proposed use of resources. SW4P provides the settlement role. Where a programme uses allocation, claims, rewards, payout schedules, reserves, or reconciliation, those capabilities belong to SW4P Earn.

**Current status: SW4P Earn is not yet publicly available. No staking, liquidity, reward, or claim programme is currently open.**
