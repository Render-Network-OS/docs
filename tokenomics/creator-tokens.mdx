Creator tokens are a planned way for a creator-specific programme to define access, participation, or decision rights around its activities.

## Overview

A creator token would belong to a particular programme, with its own purpose and terms. Those terms would explain what participants can do with the token and how it relates to the creator’s work, community, or experiences.

[$555](/tokenomics/555-token) is RNDRNTWRK’s coordination asset. A creator token would be a separate asset whose uses are defined by its specific programme.

[Render Expand](/products/render-expand) describes the planned path for developing these creator-specific arrangements.

## Programme Terms

### Access and participation

A programme would explain which experiences or features it offers and how people take part. If token holdings are used for access, the terms would identify the token, network, amount, and access provided.

A subscription would also explain what is included, its duration, price, renewal, and cancellation.

### Decisions and authority

The programme would define who can propose changes, vote, or approve decisions, and which matters those decisions cover.

Any changes to access or commercial terms would need a clear scope and effective date so participants can understand the arrangements that apply to them.

### Funding and allocations

Programme funding, service fees, and token-related revenue have their own costs and obligations. The agreements would identify the funding source, contributors, recipients, and how amounts are allocated.

SW4P Earn extends SW4P’s economic model into source-aware allocation, claims, rewards, payout schedules, reserves, and reconciliation.

Any proposed economic staking arrangement would belong to [SW4P Earn](/products/earn). Its programme terms would explain the commitment, duration, release conditions, and how it relates to eligibility.

If a programme includes a buyback policy, its terms would identify the token, funding source, spending limits, and how decisions are made. Reserve rules would state the purpose of held funds and the conditions for using or releasing them.

**Current status: SW4P Earn is not yet publicly available. No staking, liquidity, reward, or claim programme is currently open.**

## Planning a Creator Programme

<Steps>
  <Step title="Define the programme and token">
    Describe the experience, the people involved, and the token’s purpose. Specify its name, symbol, supply approach, intended network, token standard, and decimals as part of the proposed design.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the experience">
    Identify the stream controls, media formats, and interactive activities the programme would use. Explain the access conditions and how the token would relate to participation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Organise the economics">
    Identify funding, direct costs, contributor payments, and allocation rules. SW4P provides the settlement role, while SW4P Earn provides the allocation, claim, and payout model.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish the terms and review the programme">
    A future programme would present its features, participation terms, and decision rules before people join. It would explain how changes are proposed and communicated, and how programme results are reviewed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Discovery and Advertisers

A creator profile could explain the programme’s formats, placements, audience or community, and reported results. Each measure would need its source, reporting period, and meaning.

Commercial partners could use that information to assess whether the programme fits their brief. The agreement would define the deliverables, budget, measurement, and payments for the work.
