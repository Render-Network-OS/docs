A distribution needs a clear purpose, funding source, eligible recipients, and rules for receiving it.

## Airdrops and Earned Rewards

An airdrop distributes an asset to a defined set of recipients. An earned reward is based on activity or a contribution that meets a programme’s rules.

A programme would explain how recipients are selected, what they receive, and how the distribution takes place. Those details connect the reason for an allocation to the process for receiving it.

## Funding and Allocation

A funded incentive uses a budget designated for that programme. A fee-derived allocation comes from a specified fee source under its allocation rules, after accounting for the applicable costs and obligations.

Keeping these sources distinct makes it possible to explain what funds a distribution and which rules apply to it.

SW4P Earn extends SW4P’s economic model into source-aware allocation, claims, rewards, payout schedules, reserves, and reconciliation.

[SW4P Earn](/products/earn) keeps the programme’s funding, eligibility, recipients, claim process, reserved amounts, and payment records connected.

**Current status: SW4P Earn is not yet publicly available. No staking, liquidity, reward, or claim programme is currently open.**

## Before Participating

A future programme would publish:

- The eligible activity and who can take part.
- The asset being distributed and its funding source.
- The allocation rules and recipients.
- The claim process and timing.
- The supported network and addresses.
- The effective date.
- The support path for questions or a claim that needs attention.

These terms would explain the relationship between a participant’s recorded activity, their eligibility, and any amount allocated to them.

## Points, Credits, and Balances

Points can record progress or participation. Credits can record a balance within an experience.

The experience’s terms explain how those records may be used, including any conversion, expiry, transfer, or payout rules. The stated conversion and claim rules determine whether a recorded balance can be paid out.
