## Binding evidence classes

- `CHAIN_VERIFIED`: current account state or a decoded Solana transaction.
- `SPL_TOKEN_BURN_INSTRUCTION`: an SPL Token `burn` or `burnChecked` instruction. Transfers to a labelled wallet do not count.
- `CANONICAL_X_PAYLOAD`: a recovered canonical RNDRNTWRK X post.
- `GMAIL_CONFIRMED_CANONICAL_URL`: a canonical X status URL preserved in connected sent mail.
- `FOUNDER_DISCLOSED`: an economic position or obligation disclosed by the founder and kept separate from chain totals until contract-level matching.
- `DEPLOYMENT_DERIVED_NOT_CASH_PAID`: an operating-cost estimate derived from observed deployment topology and published provider rates. It is not an invoice.

## Streamflow treatment

3. 5,000,000 $555 for recipient B under the same or materially similar condition;

The two 55,555,555-token 2026 CREATE/return cycles remain historical flows. They are not netted across unrelated contracts and do not prove a zero active position.

## Buyback treatment

A chain swap proves a purchase. It enters the public protocol-buyback subtotal only after project funding and intent are independently attributed, including the canonical X evidence where available. Every row retains its signature, wallet, input asset, output amount, venue and post-purchase destination.

## Burn treatment

Only SPL Token `burn` and `burnChecked` instructions enter the verified burn subtotal. Supply differences, ordinary transfers, inaccessible custody and transfers to burn-labelled addresses remain separate.

## Infrastructure treatment

The cost model separates:

1. published provider unit rates;
2. deployment-derived capacity and run rate;
3. historical invoices and settled cash;
4. variable product COGS intended to be prepaid or recovered.

The four operating states are lights-on truth, production proof, multi-chain flywheel and full-system scale. None is labelled historical cash paid without billing evidence.
